Property management SaaS with four portals: Landlord, Tenant, Service Professional and Admin. Built digital leasing, online rent payments, invoicing and maintenance workflows, integrating Stripe Connect for rent collection and payouts, Plaid for bank and identity verification, and SignNow for lease e-signatures. Now used by hundreds of users managing thousands of properties.
